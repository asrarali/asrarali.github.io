Delete your Parwar account

For the Parwar app by Parwar, on Android and iPhone

In the app

  1. Open Parwar and go to Settings (the gear on the Home screen).
  2. Tap Account.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

If you are the Head of a club that has other members, first make someone else Head (open the club → Members → the member → Make Head). A club where you are the only member is deleted with your account.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, or never signed in with Google or Apple, email UdaanKR@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Parwar account”. Tell us the email address of the Google or Apple account you signed in with, or the name you used in your clubs and the club names, so we can find your account. We reply within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account, including your Google or Apple sign-in and email address
  • Your profile: name, loft name, WhatsApp number and profile photo
  • Live tournaments you organise, with their results, photos and videos
  • Your club memberships, join requests and votes
  • Your loft sync copy and its photos, if no other phone is linked to it
  • Your Organiser plan record
  • Everything Parwar stored on the phone you delete from

Have the Organiser plan? Deleting your account does not cancel the subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions) or on iPhone (Settings → your name → Subscriptions), or it keeps renewing.

What is kept

  • Posts and replies you wrote on club boards stay in the club, with your name removed, so conversations still make sense. Your name stays in the club's rank history.
  • Results you timed as a referee in someone else's tournament stay in that tournament, because they are part of everyone's results. Photos and videos you took there are deleted from our server 30 days after the tournament ends.
  • A loft sync copy shared with other phones stays for them. To remove it for everyone, use Settings → Loft backup → Delete online copy before deleting your account.
  • Your Google Drive backup is in your own Drive. Delete it there (Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Parwar → Delete hidden app data).
  • The store and RevenueCat keep their record of any purchase, as stores must. Ask us and we will have RevenueCat delete theirs.

Deletion takes effect immediately and cannot be undone. We keep no separate archive.
