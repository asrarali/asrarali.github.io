Terms of use

Last updated 24 October 2026

In short: Parwar is free to use. Organisers who want to run more or bigger live tournaments can buy the Organiser plan, a monthly subscription through Google Play or the App Store that renews until you cancel it. Be fair and respectful in clubs and tournaments.

Agreeing to these terms

These terms are between you and Parwar (“we”) and cover the Parwar app for Android and iPhone. By using the app you agree to them. How we handle your data is in the privacy policy.

Using Parwar

The loft book, guides, clubs, following tournaments and refereeing are free. You may use Parwar without an account; signing in with Google or Apple lets you keep your account when you change phones. You are responsible for what happens on your phone and in your account.

The Organiser plan

What it gives you

Without the plan, you can run one live tournament at a time (from the day you put it online until its last day), with up to 10 lofts. With the plan, you can run any number at the same time, with up to 100 lofts each. The plan screen in the app always shows the current limits before you buy. Tournaments kept only on your phone are never limited.

Price and payment

The price is shown in the app and in the store, in your currency, before you confirm. Payment is taken by Google Play or the App Store and charged to your store account. Taxes may apply as the store shows them.

Renewal and cancelling

  • The plan renews automatically every month at the same price, until you cancel it.
  • Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge: in Google Play, Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions; on iPhone, Settings → your name → Subscriptions. The app's plan screen has a Manage subscription button that opens the right page.
  • After cancelling, the plan stays on until the end of the month you paid for.
  • If the store changes the price, it tells you first and, where the law or the store requires, asks you to agree.
  • Deleting the app or your Parwar account does not cancel the subscription.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by the store you paid, under its rules: Google Play refunds or Apple refunds. We cannot refund store payments ourselves.

When the plan ends

Nothing you made is taken away. Tournaments you started with the plan keep their lofts and can still take more, up to 100. You go back to the free limits only for new tournaments.

Your account and other phones

The plan belongs to your Parwar account, so sign in with Google or Apple before buying. On a new phone, sign in with the same account and tap Restore purchases. A plan bought on Android cannot be managed from an iPhone, or the other way round.

Your content

What you add to Parwar (names, photos, posts, tournaments and results) stays yours. You let us store it and show it to the people the app lets see it, as described in the privacy policy, for as long as it is kept there.

Fair use

Please do not:

  • post anything illegal, abusive, hateful or sexual, or share other people's private information without their permission;
  • enter false landing times or photos, or interfere with someone else's tournament;
  • use Parwar to organise gambling or betting where that is against the law;
  • try to get into accounts, clubs or tournaments that are not yours, or to break or overload the app.

Club Heads and Deputies can remove posts and members in their clubs. We may remove content, or suspend an account, that breaks these rules.

Tournaments and results

Parwar is a tool for recording and sharing tournaments. The organiser sets the rules and is responsible for the tournament, including any prizes; we are not part of any agreement between organisers and flyers. Times are taken from referees' phones, and photos, videos and locations are there to help people check them. We do our best to keep results accurate, but we cannot guarantee them, and disputes are for the organiser to settle.

The service

We keep improving Parwar and may change or remove features. Much of it works offline, but online features need an internet connection and the services that run them, and may sometimes be unavailable. Tournament photos and videos are removed from our server 30 days after a tournament ends, so save the ones you want to keep.
