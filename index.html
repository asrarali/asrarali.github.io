Udaan

اڑان · उड़ान

Tournaments, loft book and ustad knowledge for high-flying pigeon fanciers.

Udaan is a phone app for the shauqeen who fly high-flying pigeons in Pakistan, India and the Gulf. It works in Urdu, Hindi, Arabic and English, and most of it works without signal.

Live tournaments

Organisers run a tournament, referees time each loft's release and landings, and everyone follows the leaderboard live.

Loft book

Keep your birds, pairs, clutches and care reminders, and back them up to share with other phones.

Clubs

Run a club with a Head, Deputies and ranks, a notice board, events and club tournaments.

Ustad knowledge

Guides on flying, feeding and caring for high flyers, with sunrise and sunset times for your city.

Your account

You can use Udaan without signing up. To keep your clubs when you change phones, sign in with Google or Apple in Settings → Account.
