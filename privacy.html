Privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

In short: Parwar keeps your birds and records on your phone. What you put online (tournaments, clubs, loft sync, your profile) is stored so the right people can see it. Backups go to your own Google Drive, not to us. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell your data. You can delete your account and its data at any time.

Who we are

Parwar (“we”) is an app for high-flying pigeon fanciers. This policy covers the Parwar app for Android and iPhone. Questions: UdaanKR@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Your birds, pairs, clutches, flights, health notes, care reminders, bird photos and tournaments you have not put online are kept only on your phone. They leave it only if you back them up, turn on loft sync or put a tournament online. Care reminders are local notifications made on the phone; no server is involved, and Parwar does not use push notifications.

What is stored online, and who sees it

Online features use Supabase, a cloud database and file store that we use as our server. The first time you use an online feature, the app creates a private account for you without a password.

Backups

Google Drive backup saves your loft book, settings, tournaments and photos (and videos, if you choose) to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. Parwar asks Google only for access to that folder, not to the rest of your Drive. The backup is not sent to our server, and we cannot read it. The backup file option makes a file on your phone that you can save or share wherever you choose.

Signing in with Google or Apple

Signing in is optional. It lets you get your account back on a new phone. Google or Apple tells us your name, email address and, for Google, your profile photo. Apple may give us a private relay address instead of your email if you choose to hide it. We use these only to sign you in and to fill in your profile; you can change your name and photo in Settings. We do not get your Google or Apple password.

The Organiser plan (purchases)

The Organiser plan is an optional monthly subscription bought through Google Play or the App Store. They handle the payment; Parwar never sees your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to check purchases with the store. RevenueCat knows your Parwar account number (a random id, not your name or email), what you bought, when it renews or ends, and your phone's app and system version. See Terms for how the plan works.

Phone permissions

  • Camera and photos: to photograph your birds, take your profile photo, and take proof photos in tournaments.
  • Microphone: only for the sound of the video recorded before release.
  • Location: only while the app is open and a referee marks a landing. Parwar never tracks your location in the background. Weather and sunrise times use the city you choose, not your location.
  • Notifications: for your own care reminders and clutch dates.

You can refuse any of these; the rest of the app keeps working.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising or tracking IDs.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not sell or rent your data, or share it with anyone except the services that run the app (below).

Services that run the app

  • Supabase stores the online data described above.
  • Google and Apple handle sign-in if you choose it, and the purchase if you buy the Organiser plan. Google Drive holds your backup if you turn it on.
  • RevenueCat checks Organiser plan purchases with the store.
  • Open-Meteo provides the weather. The app sends it only the position of the city you chose from the list.
  • Expo delivers app updates.

Like any internet service, these see your phone's internet address, and some see the app and system version. Their servers may be outside your country. Data is sent to them over encrypted connections, and our database only lets each person read what the rules above allow.

How long we keep it

  • Tournament photos and videos are deleted from our server 30 days after the tournament's last day. Phones that downloaded them keep their copies.
  • Club board photos are deleted from our server 180 days after they are posted, or when the post is deleted.
  • Results (release and landing times, locations and positions) are kept until the tournament is deleted or its organiser deletes their account, so results stay checkable. Board post text is kept until the post's club is deleted.
  • A deleted board post is hidden from the club. Its text stays stored, readable only by its author and the club's Head and Deputies, until the club is deleted.
  • Answered join requests are deleted after 90 days.
  • Everything else is kept until you delete it or delete your account.

Things you shared with others, such as club posts and results you timed as a referee, may stay after your account is deleted, without your account attached, so conversations and results still make sense.

Deleting your data

If you signed in with Google or Apple: Settings → Account → Delete my account. This deletes your account, profile and photo, the tournaments you organise, your club memberships and your Organiser plan record, and erases the app's data on your phone. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in Google Play or the App Store. If you never signed in, or no longer have the app, email us. See Delete your account for details.

To erase only what is on your phone, use Settings → Erase all data on this phone, or uninstall the app. To delete a Google Drive backup, remove Parwar's data in Google Drive (Settings → Manage apps).

Children

Parwar is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Important changes will also be shown in the app.
