Privacy policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

In short: Parwar keeps your birds and records on your phone. What you put online (tournaments, clubs, loft backup, your profile) is stored so the right people can see it. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell your data. You can delete your account and its data at any time.

Who we are

Parwar (“we”) is an app for high-flying pigeon fanciers. This policy covers the Parwar app for Android and iPhone. Questions: UdaanKR@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Your birds, pairs, clutches, care reminders, notes, bird photos and tournaments you have not put online are kept only on your phone. They leave it only if you turn on Loft backup or put a tournament online. Care reminders are local notifications made on the phone; no server is involved.

What is stored online, and who sees it

Online features use Supabase, a cloud database and file store that we use as our server. The first time you use an online feature, the app creates a private account for you without a password.

Signing in with Google or Apple

Signing in is optional. It lets you get your account back on a new phone. Google or Apple tells us your name, email address and, for Google, your profile photo. Apple may give us a private relay address instead of your email if you choose to hide it. We use these only to sign you in and to fill in your profile; you can change your name and photo in Settings. We do not get your Google or Apple password.

Phone permissions

  • Camera and photos: to photograph your birds, take your profile photo, and take proof photos in tournaments.
  • Microphone: only for the sound of the video recorded before release.
  • Location: only while the app is open and a referee marks a landing. Parwar never tracks your location in the background.
  • Notifications: for your own care reminders and clutch dates.

You can refuse any of these; the rest of the app keeps working.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising or tracking IDs.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not sell or rent your data, or share it with anyone except the services that run the app (below).

Services that run the app

  • Supabase stores the online data described above.
  • Google and Apple handle sign-in if you choose it.
  • Expo delivers app updates. Like any download, this shares your phone's internet address and app version with Expo.

These servers may be outside your country. Data is sent to them over encrypted connections, and the database only lets each person read what the rules above allow.

How long we keep it

Online data is kept until you delete it (for example a post or a tournament you organise) or delete your account. Things you shared with others, such as club posts, may stay in the club without your name after your account is deleted, so conversations still make sense.

Deleting your data

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete my account. This deletes your account, profile and photo, the tournaments you organise and your club memberships, and erases the app's data on your phone. See Delete your account for details, or to ask us to delete it for you.

To erase only what is on your phone, use Settings → Erase all data on this phone, or uninstall the app.

Children

Parwar is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Important changes will also be shown in the app.
